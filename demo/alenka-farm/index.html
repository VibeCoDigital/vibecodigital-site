All Natural and Organic - the main reason behind our farm

The main purpose of our farm is to raise all-natural, pesticide- and hormone-free animals and birds. We let our poultry walk outside every day, which is why their eggs are so delicious.

Free Range Chickens

We love our chickens! They are truly free-range, going outside every day to look for insects and fresh greens. We have several chicken breeds, and their eggs come in white, brown, blue, and green colors.

Best friends and helpers

King Rex

Our Great Pyrenees are free-range livestock guardian dogs. They do not know a leash or chain. They are very strong and healthy, running around 22 acres of farm. A few years ago we had many problems with our chickens and rabbits because of foxes and coyotes. Now there are no coyotes or foxes around! Our chickens and ducks are walking in the woods and coming back in the evening and they all are alive!

For the last two years, we did not lose any birds to predators! You are welcome to visit our farm in WV and observe all our animals.

Hedgehogs

We have several African hedgehogs. They are almost white in color. Here are a few interesting facts about these nocturnal animals. Each hedgehog has around 5,000 spines — each of which lasts about a year.

As they move through the hedges looking for worms and insects, they make a piggy grunting noise. Hence, hedgehog.

They will eat slugs, beetles, caterpillars, etc., and do no harm to gardens.

Hedgehogs are not affected by snake venom.

Chickens and Ducks

Live Roosters

Price for roosters depends on size, breed, and age. Usually it is between $10 and $25.

Live Peking Ducks (males)

Our ducks roam in our pastures eating bugs and grubs in the soil, and are also offered a non-GMO supplement, which contains grains like corn, soy, barley, and wheat. They never receive antibiotics, artificial growth hormones, or steroids. Price for large live duck $15.
