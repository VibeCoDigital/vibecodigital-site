About Exotic and Native Animals, Inc.

Exotic and Native Animals, Inc. (ENA) is committed to its mission: rehabilitation and releasing to the wild injured, ill, or orphaned native WV birds and animals, and conserving and restoring natural ecosystems for the benefit of humanity and the Earth's biological diversity.

What We Do

ENA advances its mission through demonstrations of exotic animals to adults and K-12 students, researching optimal conditions for wild bird breeding, reproduction, and the release of native quails and pheasants into nature.

Our team

We are wildlife enthusiasts, living far away from big cities, in the mountains of West Virginia. We own 22 acres of land here and already have several breeds of exotic and native animals and birds.

Our closest neighbors have hundreds, even thousands, of acres of land, which are mostly wooded mountains with scattered hay fields. This creates ideal conditions for wildlife restoration.

Work with Children

In addition to accepting visitors at the farm, we sometimes make trips to local schools. This way we can show our animals and birds to a large number of kids at once. So, recently we visited West Virginia Schools for the Deaf and the Blind and Romney Elementary School. Below are some pictures from these visits and visits to our farm. At Romney Elementary School we even brought a small incubator with fertilized chicken and duck eggs. Children were able to watch the entire process from incubating to hatching and feeding newborn chicks.
