Track the Track

Real-time DC Metro arrivals, right in your pocket. Know exactly when your train is coming, which platform to use, and whether the escalators are even working.

No login. No tracking. No fluff — just the Metro info you actually need, updated every 25 seconds.

  • Live arrivals for all 97 stations, updated every 25 seconds
  • Service alerts and active incident tracking system-wide
  • Interactive Metro map with station search
  • Advanced Mode: weather, line reliability scores, urgency indicators
  • Set a home station — your commute is always one tap away
  • Escalator & elevator outage status per station
  • Zero personal data collected — everything stays on your device
